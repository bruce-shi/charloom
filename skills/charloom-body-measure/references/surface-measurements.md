# Arm length and arm-to-waist clearance

Measure each side independently on the original scan and the **exported skin** in the same frame. Record shoulder–elbow, elbow–wrist and wrist–visible fingertip lengths in 3D, their sum, the vertical drop from shoulder to fingertip, and the fingertip's horizontal offset. Locate the hand endpoint on the surface. A wrist joint, palm centre or terminal bone can sit well above the fingertip. On a gloved scan, identify which fingertip is visible and document the glove as a surface reference. When the upper arm and forearm agree but the endpoint does not, inspect the wrist-to-hand mapping. Fit palm length, girth, finger spread and direction separately. Check the silhouette and the wrist transition after re-export.

On the registered front and the rest export, pick matching heights at the lower ribs, navel and waist. At each height measure horizontally from the torso's outer skin edge to the same-side arm's inner skin edge. Use triangle-plane sections on the export, isolating the arm and torso faces. Compare that gap in millimetres alongside the limb lengths. For a pose-dependent gap, reproduce the reported clip and time, track heights relative to the pelvis, and measure again across the cycle. A static A-pose and a walk frame have different shoulder angles. If the rest sections agree but the moving arm hangs wide, inspect the shoulder rotations in that clip. After adjusting the swing, test the forearm and hand against the waist, hip and thigh over the cycle; check front and oblique views in Gallery.

### Measure and test the exported skin

Draw the bilateral measurements on the character's registered contour-point chart. Use the original scan wherever the skin is visible. Identify the shoulder tip, outer elbow, wrist, fingertip, hip, outer knee and outer ankle. Establish the width and centreline on the front view and the corresponding sagittal positions on the calibrated profile. Resolve occlusion on the opposite side or from scan geometry. Record any inferred span under a glove, sleeve, armour or trousers. Define the wrist reference and the proximal leg endpoint before fitting.

1. Trace the shoulder tip over the outside of the elbow to the outer wrist and the lateral hip over the outer knee to the outer ankle. Sample along the surface, including the bends. Draw these paths on the measurement chart and add up their **3D** segment lengths. Use shoulder–elbow–wrist and hip–knee–ankle joint chains as separate anatomical measurements. Record wrist-to-fingertip and shoulder-to-hip in 3D. Label height differences separately as vertical offsets. For an actual garment pattern, record the requested cuff, waistband, hem and ease separately from these body measurements.
2. Use each reference path during fitting. Compare its stations individually and inspect the shoulder and elbow, knee and ankle, palm breadth and fingertips. A matching total can conceal an error in either segment. If the reference only supplies one view, record the projected length and the missing depth.
3. Identify the corresponding locations on the final skin. Bind a reproducible path using triangle IDs and barycentric coordinates on the reviewed topology. Use mesh-derived centres or vertex bindings for anatomical stations. A copied skeleton can keep its original bone lengths even when the fitted skin is displaced from those joints.
4. Run `scripts/mh-check-lengths.py GUIDE POINTS.json EXPORTED.glb --out REPORT.json --tolerance-mm T` with the CV interpreter. The input fields are documented in the script. Supply front pixel and 3D evidence for the landmarks plus ordered surface paths. Select T from camera resolution, marking uncertainty and export scale. Read each target, actual and signed residual. Run the same review on the prior and replacement files to verify that it catches the reported mismatch. Reopen the exact export in Gallery and check profile, back, walking and isolated equipment.

**投影跨度的可比性**。把同一配准相机下渲染的最终裸身轮廓叠在量体图上。前视足部横线可以同时穿过不同前后位置的脚趾和脚跟；侧视掌缘可能包含向外张开的拇指。分清整段投影的边缘与一个局部平面的截面。将标为掌主体的参考区域与导出手掌作比较时，逐方向检查拇指是否进入网格截面。颈根斜截面也可能横跨下颌和斜方肌。记录实测差值、面片选区和不一致的解剖边界。遇到这种情况，另列同一相机中的轮廓差，并保留局部厚度或宽度项待有可辨认的同站端点后复核。
