# Neck and jaw

Trace the actual lower jaw, throat and nape from front, profile and below. Hide hair and the collar to expose the skin. At the front, follow the facial surface to the underside of the chin. Behind the ears, follow the inner skin boundary. Fused hair or a collar can give a second intersection on the same ray. Where hair hides the nape, continue the measured neck section inside the scalp and record the inferred portion.

For a gap under the chin, connect the open body-neck loop to the traced jaw boundary around its circumference. Fill missing posterior sections locally so a long triangle cannot span the opening. For a step at the neck root, blend the transition into the shoulder slope over the adjacent rows of vertices. Pin the observed jaw, preserve the clavicle and soften only the discontinuity. Check face winding and share compatible shading normals and weights across joined skin. Turn the head both ways and sample dialogue. Check paired seam vertices **and** look for holes, spikes and folds beside them under grazing light.

For a visible skin-tone break, compare the exposed cheek, ear, neck and upper chest in base colour and again under the Gallery lighting. Sample intact skin away from beard, hair, cloth and baked shadow. Decode sRGB albedo to linear colour before matching it to glTF vertex colours or a base-colour factor. Inspect roughness, metallic and normal maps under the same light. If the stripe survives a neutral material, compare the rest and posed normals on both sides of each seam as well as the vertex positions. Tint the fitted skin to representative exposed skin, retaining its texture detail. Match the upper rim to the adjoining jaw texels and the lower rim to the body's UV samples. Confine beard pigment and its high-frequency detail to the observed edge. Carry skin detail through the transition. Check both boundaries after export.

For a fully clothed scan, inspect the first bare export before head assembly. When the torso provides insufficient observed skin, the bake extends an area-weighted median of the reviewed skin surfaces into covered regions. Verify the exposed face and neck selections, inspect the bare chest and limbs in the authored material, and compare the same view in clay to identify any separate shape defect.
