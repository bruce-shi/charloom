# Fit and measure a MakeHuman body

English · [简体中文](mh-body-fitting.zh-CN.md)

Keep all observations in the scan’s working frame. Record a target for each side and anatomical station before fitting. Use visible scan skin where available. For body regions covered by clothing, align the reference to the same pose, view, scale and anatomical height. Keep estimates and their tolerances with the measurements. [Body measurement skill](../skills/charloom-body-measure/SKILL.md)

Start the fit from the complete body. Compare front, side and oblique silhouettes and the actual width and depth of the cross-sections. At the shoulder, raising one arm should preserve the rib cage and the transition from deltoid to upper arm. At the wrist, measure the palm direction as well as the wrist-to-fingertip length. [Body fitting skill](../skills/charloom-body-fit/SKILL.md)

Repeat the measurements on the exported skin and the dressed character. For a crossing, identify the affected triangles and test the local correction both at rest and in motion. Check silhouette and tissue thickness alongside crossing counts. Keep measured closed sections, partial observations and missing views distinct in the report. [Surface measurement definitions](../skills/charloom-body-measure/references/surface-measurements.md)
